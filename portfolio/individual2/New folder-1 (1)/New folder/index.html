Our Story

Made for Music LoversGood records, good music,
good times.

Vinyl Vault started with a love for music and a collection of old records. We wanted to create a place where people could find their favorite albums and discover something new.

We carry a variety of music, including rock, jazz, pop, and funk. Whether you have been collecting records for years or are just getting started, there is something here for you.

Our goal is to give music fans a friendly place to shop, talk about their favorite artists, and enjoy the experience of listening to records.

EST. 2020

What We Do
