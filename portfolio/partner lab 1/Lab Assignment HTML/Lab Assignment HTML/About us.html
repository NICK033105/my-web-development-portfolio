About Notely

Small team. Big ideas. Even bigger love for notebooks.

Designed by students, for students.

Our Story

Notely started with a simple idea: everyone deserves a great notebook. As students ourselves, we were tired of choosing between notebooks that were too expensive and ones that fell apart after a week. So we decided to create our own—affordable, durable, and designed with real people in mind.

What began as a small class project quickly turned into something bigger. We started sketching covers, testing paper, and asking friends what they actually wanted in a notebook. The answer was clear: bold designs, quality pages, and prices that make sense on a student budget.

Our Mission

We believe that ideas matter, and the tools you use to capture them should be just as reliable. Our mission is to provide high-quality, eco-friendly notebooks at prices that work for students, professionals, and creatives alike.

What We Value

  • Quality First – Every notebook is tested for durability and paper performance.
  • Sustainability – We use recycled and responsibly sourced materials whenever possible.
  • Community – A portion of our profits supports literacy programs in local schools.

Meet the Team

We're a small team of students, designers, and notebook enthusiasts who care deeply about what we make.
